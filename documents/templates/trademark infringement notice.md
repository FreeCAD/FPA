### What you can do

FreeCAD is free and open-source software.
You can use, change, and distribute the FreeCAD source code if you obey its license (LGPL).
The license does not give you the right to use the FreeCAD name or logo as a name for your own product or service.

You can use the FreeCAD name to tell the truth about your product.
For example, you can say that your product is "based on FreeCAD" or "for use with FreeCAD".
The FreeCAD brand guidelines give the full rules: <https://fpa.freecad.org/handbook/process/logo>.

If you think that this notice is an error, tell us before the date above.
If you think that you have permission, send us a copy of that permission.
We prefer to solve this problem by agreement.
