## Transfer of freecadweb.org

freecadweb.org is currently registered through Vautron Rechenzentrum AG (IANA 1443). Domain registration expires August 29, 2026 10:30:19 UTC

For .org, PIR then provides 30 days of Redemption Grace Period + 5 days of pending deletion after the registrar actually deletes the domain. PIR explicitly says to calculate public availability by adding 35 days to the registrar-deletion/last-update date.

We expect a drop-catching service to purchase the domain automatically the instant it becomes available and have decided *not* to attempt to contract with such a service ourselves (the value of the domain is very unclear). Instead we will wait to see who owns it after the fact and determine the next course of action (which may be to drop the issue entirely).
